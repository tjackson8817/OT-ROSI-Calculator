1. Asset Criticality Assessment

Industry downtime figures are for a whole site going down. An isolated line with a backup path takes out only a small part of that; a single point of failure that stops the whole plant takes out all of it. These four questions set the Site Impact Factor: the share of the site's hourly downtime cost an incident on this asset causes.

2. Loss Exposure Inputs

Single Loss Expectancy (SLE) = Downtime Cost per Hour × Disruption Duration × Recovery Cost Multiplier × Site Impact Factor (from Section 1).

How long a typical incident disrupts production, from detection through restart.
Total incident cost is typically 1.5×-3× the direct production loss once forensics, overtime, and penalties are included (up to 3-5× for major OT incidents).

Disclaimer: Downtime-cost defaults come from published surveys where one exists (Siemens True Cost of Downtime 2022 and 2024; ABB Value of Reliability 2023) and are marked as illustrative where none does -- see the note under the field. They describe a whole site, so the Site Impact Factor scales them to the asset you are assessing. Annual Rate of Occurrence and mitigation-effectiveness percentages have no reliable public industry benchmark and must be estimated from your own incident history, your cyber insurance broker, or a structured risk assessment (e.g. a FAIR-based analysis). Treat the output as a defensible starting estimate for a conversation, not a certified financial determination.
